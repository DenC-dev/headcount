How Headcount handles your work

Vote counts, meeting notes and client lists are sensitive. This page explains what Headcount does with them, in plain terms.

Sign-in

  • People sign in with an email and password. Depending on how your Headcount is set up, they can also use a Google or Microsoft account or their firm's own login page. Passwords are scrambled before they are stored, so nobody, including us, can read them. You stay signed in for up to 30 days, and you can sign out on every device at once.
  • Two-step sign-in: anyone can ask for a code from an app on their phone (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or Authy) each time they sign in on a new device, with spare one-time codes in case they lose the phone. Someone who gets into your email still cannot get past it. On Team and Firm, an admin can require it for everyone in the workspace.
  • On the Firm plan, a workspace can have everyone sign in through the firm's own login page (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and others), and require it for everyone except owners, so an owner can always get in to fix settings.
  • Every request is checked to make sure it really comes from a signed-in person who is allowed to see that workspace or campaign.

Your calendar and email

  • Opening a meeting request in Gmail, Outlook or your email app needs no permission. Headcount opens a new message and you send it yourself.
  • If your Headcount is set up for Google or Microsoft sign-in, it can also send requests and calendar invitations for you. It asks for that access the first time.
  • That permission stays in your browser tab. It is never sent to or kept by Headcount; your browser talks to Google or Microsoft directly.
  • Headcount reads your calendar only to suggest open times and check replies to invitations it sent. It sends only the messages you ask it to send, and never reads your email.

Who sees what

  • Every workspace has owners, admins, members and guests. Guests see only the campaigns they are added to.
  • A campaign can be open to the whole workspace or closed to the people added to it. View-only access is available for clients and partners.
  • Admins see an activity log of changes to people, campaigns, clients, sign-in settings and billing.

Your data

  • Everything sent between your browser and Headcount is encrypted (HTTPS), and keys and passwords for connected services are stored encrypted.
  • Headcount keeps a backup of everything every day.
  • Admins can export all of a workspace's data at any time, and delete the workspace. People can delete their own account.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach Headcount.

Services Headcount relies on

  • Google, when people sign in with a Google or Microsoft account or their firm's login page.
  • Stripe, for subscriptions and payments.
  • An email delivery service, for invitations, notifications and digests.
  • Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI, when you ask AI to draft a bill brief (the bill details and any text you paste) and when the Monitor ranks what it found (the titles and summaries of public bills, rules and notices, with your watch's priorities).
  • Congress.gov, Open States, the Federal Register and SAM.gov, which receive the bill numbers you look up and your watches' search terms; LDA.gov and USAspending.gov, which receive the bill numbers and agencies you ask about.
  • The U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup, which receives the address a supporter enters on an action page, to find their districts.
