Security

How Headcount handles your work

Vote counts, meeting notes and client lists are sensitive. This page explains what Headcount does with them, in plain terms.

Sign-in

  • Sign-in runs on Google Cloud Identity Platform. People sign in with a Google or Microsoft account, or through their firm's identity provider.
  • On the Firm plan, a workspace can connect a SAML or OIDC identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and others) and require it for everyone except owners, who keep access to fix settings.
  • Every request to the server carries a signed token that the server checks against Google's published keys.

Your calendar and email

  • Headcount asks for calendar and email access only when you first send a meeting request or invitation.
  • The access token stays in your browser tab. It is never sent to or stored on Headcount's servers; your browser talks to Google or Microsoft directly.
  • Headcount reads your calendar only to suggest open times and check replies to invitations it sent. It sends only the messages you ask it to send, and never reads your email.

Who sees what

  • Every workspace has owners, admins, members and guests. Guests see only the campaigns they are added to.
  • A campaign can be open to the whole workspace or closed to the people added to it. View-only access is available for clients and partners.
  • Admins see an activity log of changes to people, campaigns, clients, sign-in settings and billing.

Your data

  • Traffic to Headcount is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Admins can export all of a workspace's data at any time, and delete the workspace. People can delete their own account.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach Headcount.

Services Headcount relies on

  • Google Cloud Identity Platform, for sign-in.
  • Stripe, for subscriptions and payments.
  • An email delivery service, for invitations, notifications and digests.
  • Anthropic, when you ask Claude to draft a bill brief (the bill details and any text you paste) and when the Monitor ranks what it found (the titles and summaries of public bills, rules and notices, with your watch's priorities).
  • Congress.gov, Open States, the Federal Register and SAM.gov, which receive the bill numbers you look up and your watches' search terms; LDA.gov and USAspending.gov, which receive the bill numbers and agencies you ask about.
  • The U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup, which receives the address a supporter enters on an action page, to find their districts.

Reporting a problem
