How it works

Every vote, already counted.

Type the bill number and pick the legislature; the chamber comes from the bill number. Headcount loads every current member with their office and staff, reads the bill's record on Congress.gov or Open States, and fills in sponsors, recorded votes and the committee. It checks again every day. Anyone you've already talked to stays exactly as you marked them.
