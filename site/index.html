Count the votes on your bill

Headcount lists every member of the chamber with their office, phone and staff. You mark where each one stands. It estimates the odds the bill passes, shows who is worth a meeting, and drafts the request for you to send from Gmail, Outlook or your own email app.

{{trialCta}}How it works

Free for one or two people. No card needed.

Members and staff come from Congress.gov, the House staff directory, the Florida Senate and House websites, and Open States for every other state. Bills, rules and contracts come from Congress.gov, Open States, the Federal Register and SAM.gov.

How people use it

  1. Start a campaign

    Name the bill and pick the chamber. The current members load with their offices and phones, and with their staff for the U.S. House and the Florida Legislature.

  2. Mark positions

    Set each member to Yes, Lean yes, Undecided, Lean no or No as you hear from them. The odds and the list of who to see next update as you go.

  3. Ask for meetings

    The request goes to the scheduler or the right aide, offers three times, and opens in your email ready to send.

  4. Keep a record

    Log calls and meetings as they happen. The quarterly activity report lists them by client and issue for your lobbying disclosure.

What's in it

headcount / vote count

The count

  • Estimated odds of clearing committee and the floor, from each member's position
  • Simple majority, three-fifths or two-thirds, whether you want the bill passed or stopped
  • What moved this week, and the members whose vote would change the odds most
headcount / meeting request

Meeting requests

  • Addressed to the scheduler or aide from the official staff list, where the chamber publishes one
  • Opens in Gmail, Outlook or your email app, ready to send
  • When they reply, send a calendar invitation and set a follow-up
headcount / bill brief

Bill briefs

  • Type the bill number and the sponsors, status and official summary fill in
  • Add your position and talking points, or have AI draft them if it's turned on
  • For federal bills, who else is lobbying on it, from lobbying disclosure filings
headcount / monitor

Monitor

  • New bills in Congress and the states, federal rules and federal contract notices that match your words
  • Track a bill and get an email when it moves
  • Start a campaign from any bill on the list
headcount / portfolio

Portfolio

  • Each campaign's odds, count and next vote
  • Meetings requested and booked, and follow-ups that are overdue
  • Add clients as guests so they see their own campaigns

AI, only if you want it

On your phone

Add it to your home screen. It keeps working on a weak signal and catches up when you're back online.

Where the member lists come from

U.S. House

Every current member, with office phones and the staff listed in the House's own directory: names, titles and phone numbers.

U.S. Senate

All 100 senators with their offices. The Senate doesn't publish a staff directory, so you add the staff you know.

Florida Legislature

The Senate and House for the 2026–2028 term, from flsenate.gov and flhouse.gov, with legislative and district aides. Checked weekly.

Other states

Current members from Open States. Staff aren't included; you can add them by hand or paste a list.

No official directory lists staff email addresses. Headcount writes them in the format each chamber uses, like first.last@mail.house.gov, and labels them so you know to check.

For a team

Roles

Owners and admins manage people and the plan. Members work the counts. Client guests see only the campaigns they're added to.

Signing in

Passwords with optional two-step sign-in, which you can require. On the Firm plan, staff can sign in through your firm's login, such as Okta or Microsoft Entra ID.

Records

A log of who changed what, the quarterly activity report, and a full export of your data whenever you want it.
