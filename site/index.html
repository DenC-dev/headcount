How it works

From the first count to the quarterly report

  1. Count

    Load the U.S. House, the U.S. Senate or a state chamber with office contacts, and mark where each member stands.

  2. Prioritize

    Headcount ranks the members whose movement would change the outcome most, counting committee seats and who takes cues from whom.

  3. Meet

    Ask the scheduler for a meeting from your own Gmail or Outlook with open times from your calendar, then send the invitation when they reply.

  4. Report

    Log each contact as it happens. The activity report groups a quarter's contacts by client and issue for your disclosure filings.

The count, and what it means

Every seat in the chamber, colored by position, with the exact odds of clearing committee and the floor. Switch the goal to defeat a bill and every number turns around.

  • Simple majority, three-fifths or two-thirds
  • Committee and floor counted separately
  • Trend and the week's movement at a glance

Meetings that actually get scheduled

The request goes out from your own account, to the scheduler or staffer who handles the issue, with three open times from your calendar. When they answer, the invitation goes out from Google Calendar or Outlook with a Meet or Teams link.

  • Replies tracked: accepted, declined, waiting
  • A follow-up is set for every request
  • Your calendar and email access stays in your browser

A brief for every bill

Look up a federal bill on Congress.gov or a state bill on Open States to fill in sponsors, status and the official summary. Write the talking points yourself, or have Claude draft them from the bill text, clearly marked for checking.

  • Likely objections with responses
  • Prints cleanly as a leave-behind
  • Feeds every meeting brief and request

Every campaign and client on one page

The portfolio shows each campaign's client, odds and weekly change, the count against the votes needed, the next vote, meetings and overdue follow-ups.

  • Clients see their own campaigns as guests
  • Closed campaigns for sensitive work
  • Daily or weekly email digests
