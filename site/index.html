How it works

The count, already started.

Type the bill number and pick the chamber. Headcount loads every current member with their office and staff, then reads the bill's record on Congress.gov or Open States and fills in what it finds. It checks again every day. Anyone you've already talked to stays exactly as you marked them.

The count

Filled in from the roll call.

H.R. 3633 as Headcount read it from the House vote of July 17, 2025. Members who didn't vote, or who joined the House later, show as undecided.

  • SponsorsThe sponsor and every cosponsor are marked yes.
  • Roll callsVotes on the bill itself. Motions to recommit and other procedural votes are left out rather than guessed.
  • CommitteeThe committee that has the bill, with its members, chair and ranking member.
  • OddsEstimated from each member's position, and updated as you mark them.

Meetings

The request writes itself.

Open a member and choose Request a meeting. When they say yes, the invitation goes out from Google Calendar with a Google Meet link.

  • ToThe scheduler or the right aide, from the official staff list where the chamber publishes one
  • TimesThree to offer, from your calendar if it's connected
  • SendGmail, Outlook or your own email app
  • EmailsWritten in each chamber's usual format and labelled; firms with LegiStorm can upload its export for the real ones
