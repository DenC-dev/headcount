How to use Headcount

Five steps take you from signing up to asking a legislator's office for a meeting.

Everyone
  1. Create your account
  2. Start a campaign
  3. Mark where members stand
  4. Ask for a meeting
  5. Invite your team

When you need more: bill briefs, notes, the Monitor, contacts, supporters, reports, your phone.

The person who runs Headcount
  1. Connect email
  2. Turn on bill data

That's you if you created the first account.

1Create your account

Open Headcount, choose Sign in, then Create an account. Choose Continue with Google if you see it, or enter your name, your firm's name, your work email and a password. If a colleague sent you an invitation link, open that instead and you'll join their workspace.

You land on the Portfolio page with a short Getting started list.

2Start a campaign

A campaign is one bill or vote. Choose New campaign, pick the legislature, type the bill's number and name (or choose Search by topic and type a few words like sales tax), and say whether you want it passed or defeated. Headcount picks the chamber: the one that has the bill now, or the one its number points to (HB is a House bill, SB a Senate bill). Choose Create campaign. Starting from a bill in the Monitor skips even that.

Every current member is loaded with their office, phone and email, plus their staff from the official directory where one exists. If the name starts with the bill number, Headcount also reads the bill's record: sponsors and cosponsors are marked Yes, members who voted on it are marked the way they voted, and the committee that has it is filled in. You don't have to type any of it.

Counting a committee, or a chamber that isn't listed? Pick I'll add the members myself, then paste a list with one member per line, like Jordan Avery, D, 12.

3Mark where members stand

Each dot is a seat. Click one, or a name in the Whip count list, and set their position: Yes, Lean yes, Undecided, Lean no or No. The odds of passage update as you go.

Who to see next lists the members who would move your odds the most. Note how movable each one is under Details and the list gets sharper.

4Ask for a meeting

Open the member and choose Request a meeting. Headcount picks the right person to write to (usually the scheduler), offers three times and drafts the email. Change anything you like, then choose Open in Gmail, Open in Outlook or Open in my email app. The message opens ready to send.

When the office says yes, choose Send a calendar invitation, pick the time and choose Open in Google Calendar. The meeting opens filled in, with the office invited and a Google Meet link; click Save and Google sends the invitation. If your Headcount has Google turned on, Send invitation from Google Calendar does it in one step.

About staff email addresses: official directories list staff names, titles and phones but not their emails. Headcount writes the address the way that chamber always formats it and marks it usual format. These are right almost every time; if one bounces, call the office number shown.

5Invite your team

Open the Workspace tab. Under People, type a colleague's email, pick Member, Admin or Guest (for a client who should see only their own campaigns), and choose Create invitation. Everyone sees the same count, updated within seconds.

When you need more

Where the bill stands
The Bill brief tab opens with it: how far the bill has come, where it is now and what happens next, each committee stop with its vote, any hearing on the agenda (with Add to Google Calendar), and every official step restated in plain words. Headcount rereads the bill every few hours, shows a line under the campaign's title when it moves, and puts the move in your digest. Help, then What the words mean, explains terms like cloture or committee substitute.
Bill brief
The Bill brief tab. Choose Look up the bill, type its number (HB 123, H.R. 1234), and the sponsors, status and official summary fill in. Add your talking points.
Notes and follow-ups
Open a member, choose History and log the call or meeting. Add follow-ups with a date under Agenda.
New bills and rules
The Monitor tab, then New watch. Type a few words like broadband, pick Congress or the states, and Headcount tells you when something new matches.
Contacts
The Contacts tab, for agency staff, coalition partners and reporters. Add them one at a time or import a spreadsheet.
Supporters
In a campaign, More, then Action center. You get a page to share; supporters type their address, see their own legislators and send a letter. No account needed.
Reports
More, then Reports for a client update or the whole whip count. Workspace, then Lobbying activity report, for a quarter's contacts by client.
Your phone
Open Headcount in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen on an iPhone). It works with a weak signal.

For the person who runs Headcount

Two things to do once. Open your name in the top right, then Admin settings.

AConnect email

This lets people reset passwords and get invitations and alerts. Open Email and pick your email service.

  • Gmail: Google needs an app password, not your usual one. In your Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification, open App passwords, make one called Headcount and paste it in with your Gmail address.
  • Microsoft 365 or Outlook: enter the mailbox's address and password. If the test fails, your IT person may need to allow "Authenticated SMTP" for that mailbox.

Choose Save, then Send me a test email.

BTurn on bill data

Two free keys let Headcount look up bills, run the Monitor and load any state's legislature.

  • Congress.gov: sign up at api.congress.gov/sign-up. The key arrives by email.
  • Open States: make a free account at open.pluralpolicy.com and copy the key from your account page.

Paste both into Bill and legislator data, choose Test for each, then Save keys.

Also worth doing: choose Who can sign up (anyone, or only people you invite), turn on Two-step sign-in under your Account, and download a backup now and then from Backups.

Stuck?

If a screen says something "isn't turned on yet", the person who runs your Headcount can switch it on in Admin settings. For anything else, write to {{supportLine}}.
