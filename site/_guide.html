Getting started with Headcount

Headcount helps you see where every legislator stands on a bill, work out who to talk to next, set up the meetings and keep your team on the same page. This guide takes you from your first sign-in to your first count, one step at a time. It takes about ten minutes.

Not sure if that's you? It is if you created the first account. Everyone else can skip this part.

1Create your account

  1. Open Headcount and choose Sign in, then Create an account. If a colleague sent you an invitation link, open that link instead: it puts you straight into their workspace.
  2. Enter your name, your firm or team name, your work email and a password of at least 10 characters.
  3. Choose Create account. Headcount makes a workspace for your firm and opens your Portfolio, with a short Getting started list. Each item on it links back to this guide.

Just looking? You can try everything without an account: open the app and use the example campaign. It stays in your browser until you sign in.

2Start your first count

A campaign is one bill or vote you are working on.

  1. On the Portfolio page, choose New campaign.
  2. Type the bill's name, for example SB 100: Rural broadband, and choose whether you want it to pass or to be defeated. If you work for clients, pick the client. Choose Create campaign.
  3. Headcount asks Which chamber are you counting? Pick it, such as the U.S. Senate or a state House, and choose Load the members. Every current member appears, with their office, phone and email.

Chamber not in the list? Choose Paste your own list and paste one member per line, such as Jordan Avery, D, 12. Name is the only part you must include. A committee works the same way.

3Mark where members stand

  1. Each dot in the chamber is a seat. Choose any dot, or a name in the Whip count list, to open that member.
  2. Under Position on the bill, choose Yes, Lean yes, Undecided, Lean no or No. The dot changes color right away.
  3. Watch Odds of passage today. It updates with every change, counting the votes you need on the floor and, if you mark committee members, in committee.

Saving a step: in Edit campaign, you can set every member of one party to the same starting position at once, then adjust the few who differ.

4See who to talk to next

Who to see next lists the members whose change of mind would help you most, with how many points each could add to your odds. Members you are less likely to move appear lower.

  1. Open a member and choose Details to note how movable they are, who they follow, and what they care about. The list gets smarter as you add this.
  2. Tick members in the list to see where the odds would land if those meetings all went your way.

5Ask for a meeting

  1. Open the member and choose Request a meeting.
  2. Pick who to send it to (the office, or a staff member you've added), the length, and up to three times that suit you. Headcount writes a polite request that you can change.
  3. Choose Open in my email app to send it from your own email, or Copy message to paste it anywhere. If your Headcount is connected to Google or Microsoft, you can send it directly and offer times from your calendar.
  4. When they reply, switch to Send a calendar invitation to send the meeting itself.

Before you go, choose Meeting brief on the member for a one-page summary of the member, the bill and your talking points.

6Write the bill brief

  1. Choose the Bill brief tab.
  2. Choose Look up the bill and enter its number, such as HB 123 or H.R. 1234. Headcount fills in the sponsors, where the bill stands and the official summary.
  3. Add your own summary, talking points and the objections you expect. If your Headcount has AI writing help turned on, Draft it with AI writes a first version for you to check.

If the lookup says it isn't turned on yet, choose Write it by hand, or ask the person who runs Headcount to do step B.

7Keep notes and follow-ups

  1. After a call or meeting, open the member, choose History and log what happened. Your team sees it too.
  2. In Agenda, add follow-ups such as Send the fiscal note, with a date and the person responsible. Overdue ones show at the top of the page.

8Invite your team and clients

  1. Choose the Workspace tab. Under People, type a colleague's email (or leave it empty to make a link anyone can use) and pick their role:
    • Member: works on campaigns.
    • Admin: also manages people, clients and settings.
    • Guest: a client or coalition partner who sees only the campaigns you share with them.
  2. Choose Create invitation. If email is connected, Headcount sends it. If not, copy the link and send it yourself.
  3. Under Clients, add the organizations you represent, so each campaign, report and alert can be filed under a client.

9Watch for new bills and rules

  1. Choose the Monitor tab, then New watch.
  2. Choose what to watch (bills, new federal rules or federal contract notices), type a few search words such as broadband, rural internet, and pick where: Congress, every state, or the states you name.
  3. Describe in a sentence what matters to you. Headcount puts the most relevant results first and emails you if you like.

10Keep your contacts

The Contacts tab is for everyone who isn't a legislator in a campaign: agency staff, coalition partners, client contacts and reporters.

  1. Choose Add contact, or Import to bring in a spreadsheet saved as CSV (or paste rows copied from Excel or Google Sheets).
  2. Open a contact to log calls, meetings and emails, so the whole team sees the relationship.

11Rally supporters

  1. Open a campaign, choose More, then Action center.
  2. Write a title, why it matters, and a letter supporters can edit. Choose which legislators they can write to, then turn the page on and copy its link.
  3. Share the link by email or social media. Supporters type their address (their phone or browser can fill it in), see their own legislators, and send the letter or call. They don't need an account.
  4. Each letter shows next to that legislator in your whip count.

12Reports for clients and filings

  • More, then Reports: a client update you can paste into an email, the contact log, or the whole whip count, to copy or download.
  • Workspace, then Lobbying activity report: everyone's logged contacts for a quarter, by client and issue, to help with your disclosure filings. Check it against your records before you file.

13Use it on your phone

  1. Open Headcount in your phone's browser and sign in.
  2. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install.
  3. It opens like an app, even with a weak signal in the capitol, and catches up when you're back online.

For the person who runs Headcount

First-time setup

If you created the first account, you manage Headcount's settings. Open your name in the top right, then Admin settings. Each section has a test button, and changes take effect right away.

AConnect email

Without email, people can't reset forgotten passwords, and invitations and alerts aren't sent.

  1. In Admin settings, open Email and choose your email service.
  2. Gmail or Google Workspace: Google needs an app password, not your normal password. Turn on 2-Step Verification for the Google account, then open App passwords, make one named Headcount, and paste the 16 letters into Headcount with the Gmail address.
  3. Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com: enter the mailbox's email address and password. If the test fails, ask your IT administrator to allow "Authenticated SMTP" for that mailbox.
  4. Choose Save, then Send me a test email and check your inbox (and spam folder).

BTurn on bill and legislator data

Two free keys let Headcount look up bills, load any state legislature and run the Monitor.

  1. Congress.gov: sign up at api.congress.gov/sign-up. The key arrives by email within a minute.
  2. Open States (all 50 states): create a free account at open.pluralpolicy.com and copy the key from your account page.
  3. In Admin settings, open Bill and legislator data, paste each key, choose Test, then Save keys.
  4. Optional: a SAM.gov key adds federal contract notices to the Monitor, and an Anthropic key (under AI writing help) turns on AI drafting. Anthropic charges for what it uses.

CChoose who can sign up

In Admin settings, open Who can sign up. Choose Only people you invite if Headcount is just for your firm, or Anyone if you offer it to others.

DKeep accounts safe and backed up

  • Turn on Two-step sign-in for your own account (your name, then Account). It asks for a code from an app on your phone when you sign in on a new device. In the Workspace tab, you can require it for everyone.
  • Headcount saves a copy of everything once a day and keeps a week of copies. Under Admin settings, then Backups, download one now and then to keep somewhere safe.

Still stuck?

Most screens say what to do next in plain words. If something says it "isn't turned on yet", the person who runs your Headcount can switch it on in Admin settings (steps A and B above). For anything else, write to {{supportLine}}.
