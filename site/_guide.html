How to use Headcount

Five steps, from signing up to your first meeting request.

Everyone

  2. Start a campaign

  5. Invite your team

When you need more: bill briefs, notes, the Monitor, contacts, supporters, reports, your phone.

The person who runs Headcount
  1. Connect email
  2. Turn on bill data

That's you if you created the first account.

1Create your account

Open Headcount and choose Sign in, then Create an account (or Continue with Google). Invited by a colleague? Open their link instead.

You land on the Portfolio page with a short Getting started list.

2Start a campaign

On the Portfolio, pick the legislature and type the bill number (or a few words to search). Choose Count it. Headcount picks the chamber that has the bill now.

Every member loads with their office, phone, email and staff. Sponsors, cosponsors and recorded votes are marked from the public record, and the committee that has the bill is filled in.

Counting a committee, or a chamber that isn't listed? Pick I'll add the members myself, then paste a list with one member per line, like Jordan Avery, D, 12.

3Mark where members stand

Each dot is a seat. Click one, or a name in the Whip count list, and set where they stand. The odds update as you go.

Who to see next lists the members who would move your odds the most. Note how movable each one is under Details and the list gets sharper.

4Ask for a meeting

Open the member and choose Request a meeting. Headcount picks the staffer (usually the scheduler), offers three times and drafts the email. Choose Open in Gmail, Outlook or my email app.

When they say yes, choose Send a calendar invitation, then Open in Google Calendar. The invitation has the office and a Meet link; click Save.

Staff emails: directories don't publish them, so Headcount writes each in the chamber's usual format and labels it. If one bounces, call the office.

5Invite your team

In Workspace, under People, enter a colleague's email, pick a role (Guest for a client who sees only their campaigns) and choose Create invitation.

When you need more

Where the bill stands
At the top of Bill brief: how far the bill has come, what happens next, each committee stop and vote, upcoming hearings (with Add to Google Calendar), and every step in plain words. Headcount rechecks every few hours and puts moves in your digest.
Bills you follow
The Bills tab follows bills without a whip count. Follow a bill, mark your position, priority and client, and add private notes. Share the list makes a read-only page or website embed.
Legislators
The Legislators tab shows where each legislator stands on all your bills, how often they're with you, and your team's history with them.
Similar bills elsewhere
At the bottom of the Bill brief: search every state and Congress for the same idea, and follow what you find.
Bill brief
The Bill brief tab. Choose Look up the bill, type its number (HB 123, H.R. 1234), and the sponsors, status and official summary fill in. Add your talking points.
Notes and follow-ups
Open a member, choose History and log the call or meeting. Add follow-ups with a date under Agenda.
New bills and rules
The Monitor tab, then New watch. Type a few words like broadband, pick Congress or the states, and Headcount tells you when something new matches.
Contacts
The Contacts tab, for agency staff, coalition partners and reporters. Add them one at a time or import a spreadsheet.
Supporters
In a campaign, More, then Action center. You get a page to share; supporters type their address, see their own legislators and send a letter. No account needed.
Reports
More, then Reports for a client update or the whole whip count. Workspace, then Lobbying activity report, for a quarter's contacts by client.
Your phone
Open Headcount in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen on an iPhone). It works with a weak signal.

For the person who runs Headcount

Two things to do once. Open your name in the top right, then Admin settings.

AConnect email

This lets people reset passwords and get invitations and alerts. Open Email and pick your email service.

  • Gmail: Google needs an app password, not your usual one. In your Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification, open App passwords, make one called Headcount and paste it in with your Gmail address.
  • Microsoft 365 or Outlook: enter the mailbox's address and password. If the test fails, your IT person may need to allow "Authenticated SMTP" for that mailbox.

Choose Save, then Send me a test email.

BTurn on bill data

Two free keys let Headcount look up bills, run the Monitor and load any state's legislature.

  • Congress.gov: sign up at api.congress.gov/sign-up. The key arrives by email.
  • Open States: make a free account at open.pluralpolicy.com and copy the key from your account page.

Paste both into Bill and legislator data, choose Test for each, then Save keys.

Also: set Who can sign up, turn on Two-step sign-in, and download a backup now and then.

Stuck?

If a screen says something "isn't turned on yet", the person who runs your Headcount can switch it on in Admin settings. For anything else, write to {{supportLine}}.
