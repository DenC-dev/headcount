What Headcount stores

If you use Headcount without signing in, your campaigns stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

If you sign in, we store your name, email address and an account identifier, the workspaces you belong to, your email settings, and the campaigns you create or join: rosters, positions, notes, contact logs, staff contacts, meetings, follow-ups, bill briefs and clients. Other people in your workspace can see your name, email address and role, and the campaigns open to them. Workspace admins can see an activity log of changes to people, campaigns, clients and billing, and can export the workspace's data.

If you send the demo request form, we store what you entered and use it to reply to you.

Calendar and email

When you choose to send a meeting request or invitation, Headcount asks your permission to use your Google or Microsoft calendar and email. Headcount uses this access only to send the email or invitation you ask it to, to read your calendar to suggest open times, and to check replies to invitations it sent. It does not read, store or analyse your email. That permission stays in your browser tab and is never sent to Headcount.

Headcount's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Emails from Headcount

We email you invitations you are sent, notices when a teammate adds you to a campaign or assigns you a follow-up, billing notices if you manage a workspace, and a daily or weekly digest. You can change these in Account, Email settings, or with the unsubscribe link in each email.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We store the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the plan and the number of seats. Card details go to Stripe and are never stored by Headcount.

Bill lookup and drafting

Bill lookups send the bill number to Congress.gov or Open States. If you ask Claude to draft a brief, the bill details, your position and any text you paste are sent to Anthropic to produce the draft.

Monitoring public records

A watch sends its search terms to Congress.gov, Open States, the Federal Register or SAM.gov. To rank results, the titles and summaries of the public records found, with the watch's stated priorities, are sent to Anthropic.

Action pages

When someone takes action on a campaign's public page, Headcount stores their name, email address and the address they enter, and sends the address to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup to find their districts. Their letter, with their name and address, is emailed to their legislators' offices. Their email address is shown to the organization running the campaign only if they ask for updates.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account in the app. This removes you from every workspace. A workspace you alone own passes to its longest-standing admin or member, or is deleted if nobody else is in it. Workspace owners can delete a workspace and everything in it.
