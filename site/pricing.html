Who do we pay for?

Each owner, admin and member of the workspace. Guests, such as clients and coalition partners who only see their own campaigns, are free.

Can we add or remove people later?

Yes, any time, under Workspace, then Plan. The next bill is adjusted for the time used.

How do we pay?

{{payNote}} For invoices or an annual agreement, use the form below.

What does AI drafting do?

AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) drafts a bill brief's summary, what the bill does, talking points and likely objections from the official summary and any text you paste. Drafts are marked for checking. It's off unless whoever runs your Headcount turns it on, and each plan includes a monthly number of drafts.

Can we leave with our data?

Yes. Admins can export everything in a workspace at any time, and delete the workspace when they are done.

Questions?

Tell us which legislature you work and how your team runs counts today. We'll email you back about plans, signing in with your firm's login, or invoices, and we're happy to walk through it on a call.
