Who counts as a seat?

Owners, admins and members each use a seat. Guests, such as clients and coalition partners who only see their own campaigns, do not.

Can we change the number of seats later?

Yes. Admins change seats in the workspace settings, and the next invoice is adjusted for the time used.

How do we pay?

By card through Stripe, monthly. For invoicing or an annual agreement, use the form below.

What does drafting with Claude include?

Claude drafts a bill brief's summary, provisions, talking points and likely objections from the official summary and any text you paste. Drafts are marked for checking. Each plan includes a monthly number of drafts.

Can we leave with our data?

Yes. Admins can export everything in a workspace at any time, and delete the workspace when they are done.
