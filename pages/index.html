With an account, it starts from the public record

Start a campaign with the bill number and Headcount looks the bill up on Congress.gov or Open States:

  • Sponsors and cosponsors are marked Yes.
  • Members who voted on the bill itself are marked the way they voted. Procedural votes like motions to recommit are left out.
  • The committee that has the bill is set, with its members, chair and ranking member.
  • The bill brief gets the sponsors, status, committees and official summary.

It checks again every day. Anyone you've already talked to is left exactly as you marked them. The free browser version doesn't do this part; you mark positions yourself.

Then you work the undecided

Meeting requests

Open a member and choose Request a meeting. The request goes to the scheduler or the right aide, from the official staff list where the chamber publishes one. Headcount offers three times, drafts the email and opens it in Gmail, Outlook or your own email app. When they say yes, send the calendar invitation: Google Calendar opens with the meeting filled in and a Google Meet link added.

No official list publishes staff email addresses, so Headcount writes them in the format each chamber uses and labels them. If your firm subscribes to LegiStorm, upload its staff export and the real addresses are used instead.

Who to see next

A short list of the members who would move your odds the most, with how movable each one is and when anyone last talked to them.

Notes and follow-ups

Log a call or meeting on the member, set the follow-up, and the whole team sees it within seconds.

A brief for the meeting

Sponsors, status, committees and the official summary, filled in from the bill. Add your position and talking points, then print it or save it as a PDF.

On your phone

Where the information comes from

U.S. House

Every current member, with office phones and the staff listed in the House's own directory. Votes from the House Clerk.

U.S. Senate

All 100 senators and their offices, with votes from the Senate. The Senate publishes no staff directory, so you add the staff you know.

Florida Legislature

The Senate and House for the 2026–2028 term, from flsenate.gov and flhouse.gov, with legislative and district aides. Checked weekly.

Other states

Current members, bills, votes and committees from Open States.

Ask us

Common questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in the browser on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

What does it cost?

Trying it in your browser is free and needs no account. A free account saves your work so you can use it on any device and share it with a colleague. Paid plans for bigger teams aren't on sale yet.

Are the staff email addresses right?

No official directory publishes them, so Headcount writes each one in the format that chamber uses, such as first.last@mail.house.gov, and labels it. They're usually right. If one bounces, call the office number shown. Firms with LegiStorm can upload its export to use the real addresses.

My email app doesn't open. What now?

Use Open in Gmail or Open in Outlook instead; both open a new message in your browser with everything filled in. Copy message works with any email service.

Who can see my counts?

In the free browser version, only you. With an account, you and the colleagues you invite. Clients added as guests see only the campaigns you share with them. Your data isn't sold or shared.

Can my supporters use it?

Yes, without an account. Share a campaign's supporter page: they type their address, see their own legislators, and send a letter or call with your talking points.

Questions?

Ask anything: how it would work for your team, which legislature you work, prices. We'll answer by email.
