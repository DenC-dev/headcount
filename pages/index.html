How it works

From the first count to the quarterly report

  1. Count

    Pick the U.S. House, the U.S. Senate or any state legislature. Every current member appears, with their office; mark where each one stands.

  2. Prioritize

    Headcount tells you whose vote matters most right now, counting committee seats and who follows whose lead.

  3. Meet

    Ask the office for a meeting from your own Gmail or Outlook, offering times when you are free, and send the calendar invitation when they reply.

  4. Report

    Note each conversation as it happens. At the end of the quarter, the activity report lists them by client and issue, ready for your lobbying disclosures.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?

No. Headcount works in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone you can add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app.

What does it cost?

Trying it in your browser is free, with no account. A free account saves your work and works for two people. The Team and Firm plans are charged per person each month, after a {{trialDays}}-day free trial, and you can cancel any time.

Where does the information come from?

From official public sources: Congress.gov, all 50 state legislatures, the Federal Register, federal contract notices, lobbying disclosures, USAspending.gov and the U.S. Census. See today's status.

Who can see my counts?

Only you and the colleagues you invite. Clients you add as guests see only the campaigns you share with them. Your data is never sold or shared.

Can my supporters use it?

Yes, with no account. Share a campaign's action page: supporters type their address, see their own legislators and send a letter in their own words, or call with your talking points.

How do I pay?

By card, inside Headcount: open Workspace, then Plan. Stripe handles the payment and the receipts. You can add people, remove them or cancel whenever you like.
