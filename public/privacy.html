What Headcount stores

If you use Headcount without signing in, your campaigns stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we store your name, email address and an account identifier, the workspaces you belong to, and the campaigns you create or join: rosters, positions, notes, contact logs, staff contacts, meetings, bill briefs and clients. Other people in your workspace can see your name, email address and role, and the campaigns open to them. Workspace admins can see an activity log of changes to people, campaigns, clients and billing, and can export the workspace's data.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We store the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the plan and the number of seats. Card details go to Stripe and are never stored by Headcount.

Calendar and email

When you choose to send a meeting request or invitation, Headcount asks your permission to use your Google or Microsoft calendar and email. Headcount uses this access only to send the email or invitation you ask it to, to read your calendar to suggest open times, and to check replies to invitations it sent. It does not read, store or analyse your email. The access token stays in your browser tab and is not sent to our server.

Headcount's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bill lookup and drafting

Bill lookups send the bill number to Congress.gov or Open States. If you ask Claude to draft a brief, the bill details, your position and any text you paste are sent to Anthropic to produce the draft.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account in the app. This removes you from every campaign. Campaigns you own pass to their longest-standing editor, or are deleted if nobody else edits them.
