          How the numbers work

          Each position is treated as a chance of a yes vote on the bill: Yes 97%, Lean yes 75%, Undecided 50%, Lean no 25%, No 3%. The odds are the exact probability that the bill clears committee and then reaches the floor threshold, with every member assumed to vote the same way at both stages and to decide independently. When the goal is to defeat the bill, the page shows the chance that it fails at either stage.

          The call sheet tests each member one step in your direction, carries half of that shift to anyone marked as taking cues from them, and ranks by the gain in your odds weighted by how movable the member is. It is a planning aid built on your own reads of each member, not a forecast.
